### Concrete capability limits, not cosmetic findings

1. At the reviewed base, iOS ordinary context menus lacked Move Up/Down because
   those buttons were inside `#if os(macOS)` at `FoundationPlayerView.swift:753`.
   After coordinator granted shared-file ownership, this slice removed that guard
   and exposed the existing buttons on both platforms, preserving upcoming-only
   checks, guarded `moveAccessibleEntry`, and disabled first/last boundaries. No
   player or handle/drag change is made. Native menu verification remains pending;
   this is not a claim the accepted touch drag failed.
2. Batch selection/removal is not exposed on either queue. The player accepts a
   multi-ID reorder, but its existence does not establish a multi-selection UI or
   physical multi-drag acceptance. Do not claim that older requested check passed.
   A batch interface is a separate scope choice; do not build it speculatively.
3. Native rendered boundary, legibility, contrast, current-row clarity and scroll
   smoothness cannot be graded from this source pass. In particular, the header is
   outside the clipped/faded list; that does not prove the final rendered material
   boundary satisfies the historical owner contract. Capture open and scrolled
   states before proposing a visual change or reopening a closed visual defect.

## Exact manual sequence and evidence matrix

Use an owner-approved disposable queue. Record candidate SHA, actual version,
platform/OS, input, output route, audible result and repetition count. Do not expose
media/account IDs or raw URLs in shared evidence. Use neutral labels A–F in notes.
Preserve existing app identity/data and retained artifacts. iPhone17Pro is excluded.
Obtain the native lane from Home first; installation is a separate maintainer step.

1. Build a six-or-more occurrence queue with two copies of one track. Start first,
   advance twice, then pause: at least two History, one Current, three Upcoming.
   Note the selected duplicate occurrence using order/context, not title alone.
2. Open/close three times. Confirm immediate rows, visible current state and proper
   focus return. Scroll up to History, then down through Up Next; capture open and
   scrolled states. Confirm controls remain readable and rows disappear beneath
   their boundary. Repeat in a short Mac window and iPad split/regular widths.
3. While paused, long-press/right-click an upcoming row; cancel without action.
   Test the new iOS menu fallback: first row Move Up disabled, last row Move Down
   disabled, middle Move Up then Move Down returns the same occurrence order.
   Current/History menus must omit both moves and removal.
   Then Play Last, Play Next, remove one copy, and drag another from first to last
   and back three times. Confirm exact order and unchanged selected occurrence.
   Try dragging/menus on History/current: no edit/removal or cross-boundary move.
4. Start playback and repeat one reorder plus long-press/cancel without interruption.
   Hold a handle beyond the initial drag pickup; cancel by leaving/dismissing the
   surface. Confirm no stranded drag, accidental select, missing row or audio gap.
   Advance while dragging to exercise invalidation; confirm coherent current/order.
5. Select an upcoming duplicate, then History, paused and playing. Confirm the
   intended occurrence becomes current, list partition changes coherently and
   audible/UI/current identity agree. Verify opening itself never starts playback.
6. Shuffle On preserves History/current and upcoming membership; manually move an
   upcoming row, then Shuffle Off preserves that explicit order. Test Repeat One
   natural end, explicit Next, All wrap, and Off end on short tracks.
7. Pause, close normally, relaunch: order, occurrence and modes restore paused with
   no audio. Explicit Play begins the selected track at the beginning. Repeat once
   after editing and after process termination, recording save/lifecycle limits.
   Do not apply Handoff position expectations to this ordinary restore.
8. With audible playback and the edited queue, background/lock and return; use
   lock-screen/system Next, Previous and Play/Pause. Exercise an available approved
   route transition (built-in ↔ Bluetooth/AirPlay) and a real interruption/resume.
   Check order, selection, audible continuity and no optional-artwork wait. Mark
   unavailable hardware/route/interruption rows pending, never inferred passes.
9. VoiceOver: discover all three section headers/current trait, Shuffle/Repeat
   state, upcoming context actions, Move Up/Down and first/last limits. Repeat at
   accessibility text sizes and Reduced Transparency. Mac keyboard: ⇧⌘Q, traverse
   rows and menus with Full Keyboard Access, activate selection/modes/moves/remove,
   dismiss and recover focus. Record any unreachable action precisely.
10. Separately under #197, confirm/decline physical iPhone↔Mac Handoff using a
    populated duplicate queue and modes. Confirm preserves raw/visible membership,
    no replacement/audio before intent, paused transfer semantics and later edit/
    relaunch. Current source integration is not evidence this physical step passed.
