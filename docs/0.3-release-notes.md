# Velacanto 0.3 — native rebuild

Velacanto 0.3 is an alpha prerelease of the rebuilt Jellyfin music app. It restores the familiar browsing and playback interface around a smaller native player, with features returned through individually tested builds.

The rebuilt app replaces the legacy implementation as the single active codebase. The app is named **Velacanto** and uses the original app identifier. Existing users should expect to sign in again; legacy credentials and queue state are not migrated.

## Included

- Native audio playback, queue selection, Next/Previous, and seeking. Previous restarts the current song after three seconds; near the beginning, it selects the previous queue occurrence when available.
- Play Next and Play Last for songs, albums, and playlists. Album and artist Shuffle starts a shuffled collection.
- Jellyfin catalog and authentication through official SDK request models and native networking. Supported formats can use direct delivery; Jellyfin selects conversion when necessary.
- Home, New, Library, and Search with songs, albums, album artists, playlists, favorites, local pins, and genre browsing.
- Artist and album artwork, expandable overviews, artist appearances, similar items, and personal Most Played shelves.
- Immersive Now Playing and queue presentation. Starting a song updates the player bar without opening the full sheet.
- Native overview sheets, refined browsing headers and menus, and a progress ring on Continue Listening.
- Profile/settings with the installed version, build number, and open-source notices.

## Limitations

AirPlay selection, the in-app system-volume control, lyrics, and app-provided Control Center/Lock Screen controls are not enabled. Lyrics and AirPlay icons are disabled placeholders. The separate system-controls experiment in build 107 is excluded following a physical reliability failure.

Playback reports are not sent to Jellyfin yet. Recently Played and play-count shelves use server-recorded history and may not update from listening in this app. Most Played Albums is derived from a bounded sample of played tracks rather than an exhaustive library ranking.

Jellyfin is the only implemented provider. Offline audio, local libraries, additional providers, playlist editing, persistent shuffle/repeat modes, CarPlay, and legacy queue restoration are outside this release.

## Testing and availability

This package uses build 106 as its behavior baseline. That baseline has 55 passing automated tests and incremental owner device testing. Repackaging under the production app identity creates a new artifact; its final source, build, signing, hosted, and distribution results are recorded separately in the [acceptance record](0.3-acceptance-and-provenance.md).

Intermittent network failures observed in excluded experiments remain under investigation. Their cause is not established, and this release makes no reliability-percentage claim.

Distribution is an **alpha GitHub prerelease and internal TestFlight**. It does not promote beta, preview, or main, and it is not a public App Store release. See [known issues](0.3-known-issues.md) and the [engineering record](0.3-engineering-record.md) for scope and investigation history.
