## Evidence carried forward

The volume feature candidate had 24 passing focused Mac player tests and an iOS
build-for-testing result. Neither is physical audio or distribution evidence.
During the earlier volume-only observation, the existing journal gained zero API
or playback-error events; this does not measure native streaming or AirPlay traffic.

Owner-confirmed earlier checks included background queue advancement with audible
playback, audio-only Apple TV/HomePod output with the television remaining off,
and working lyric/queue presentation. Later direct volume testing exposed the
accepted volume defect (now deferred P2). Standalone diagnostic apps working correctly do not establish
that the combined app works correctly. macOS audible gain, VoiceOver, fixed-volume
routes and exhaustive long-duration reliability are not claimed complete here.
